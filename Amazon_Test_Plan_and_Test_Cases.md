## 2. Test Strategy, Scope & Guidelines

### In-Scope Modules (54 Event Requirements)
1. **Core Commerce Funnel:** User Auth, Product Search, Add to Cart, Checkout, Payment Gateway (Card/UPI/Net Banking), Payment Confirmation, Update Quantity, Remove from Cart.
2. **Account & Profile Management:** Profile Updates, Address Book & Communication Preferences, Order History, Return Orders & Return Centre, Wishlist, Your Account Hub.
3. **Navigation & Browsing:** Hamburger 'All' Menu, Primary Sticky Nav Bar, Amazon Logo Redirection, All Category Dropdown, Sorting Bar, Faceted Search Filters, Location Selector & Pincode Availability.
4. **Programs, Deals & Subscriptions:** Prime Membership & Benefits, Amazon Showroom (3D/AR), Trending Deals, Promotional Cashback/Coupons.
5. **Ecosystem & Auxiliary Services:** Seller Central, Amazon Appstore, Devices & Digital Content Library, Amazon Music, IMDb, AWS, Customer Support Chatbot & Call-back, FAQ and Terms of Use.
